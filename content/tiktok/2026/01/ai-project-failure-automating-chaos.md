## TLDR

Your AI project didn't fail because of the technology - it failed because you tried to automate chaos. AI doesn't fix broken processes, it scales them.

## Key Points

- AI doesn't fix broken processes - it scales them
- Paper logbooks, disconnected systems, and unstandardized data entry will all break AI
- Before deploying AI, answer honestly:
  - Are your current processes documented?
  - Have you fixed what's manually broken first?
  - Is everything digitized and standardized?
  - Can humans execute this process consistently?
- If you answered "no" to any of these, you're not ready for AI
- The real work isn't sexy: documenting, fixing, digitizing, standardizing

## Summary

Sol Rashidi shares an uncomfortable truth about AI project failures. The pattern she sees in rescue projects: companies skip the foundation work because they think AI will magically fix what's broken. They want to jump to the sexy technology part without doing the unsexy organizational cleanup first.

AI amplifies whatever you feed it. Paper logbooks? AI can't read those. Five disconnected systems? AI can't integrate those. Unstandardized data entry? AI will just amplify the inconsistency.

Most executives skip the foundation work because it's boring. That's exactly why most AI projects fail.
