## TLDR

AI receptionists deployed in property management can log maintenance calls, ask the right questions, dispatch emergency repairs, respond to resident needs, and manage leasing inquiries around the clock.

## Key Points

- AI agents can handle property management calls 24/7
- Logs maintenance requests and asks appropriate follow-up questions
- Can dispatch emergency repairs instantly
- Manages leasing inquiries automatically
- Reduces response time for resident needs

## Summary

Mason Anderson demonstrates how AI receptionists are being deployed in property management to handle routine tasks that previously required human staff.
