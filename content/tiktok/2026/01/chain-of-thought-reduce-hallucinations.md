## TLDR

Chain of thought prompting is an effective technique for reducing hallucinations in AI systems like ChatGPT and Claude by making the model reason through problems step-by-step.

## Key Points

- Chain of thought (CoT) prompting encourages AI to break down problems step-by-step
- Step-by-step reasoning reduces errors, especially in math, logic, and complex analysis
- When AI explains its reasoning, it's less likely to invent false information
- This technique works across popular AI platforms including ChatGPT and Claude
